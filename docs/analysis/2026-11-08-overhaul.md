# Neon Dash overhaul analysis — 8 Nov 2026

## What a first-time player sees (≈30 seconds)

Playing Challenge from the title screen, the first seconds are **menu-heavy**: the overlay explains spikes, gates, shields, and the color wall in several lines before **PLAY**. After starting, the opening is forgiving — pink spikes and simple jumps on a **dark runway** with skyline silhouettes and a faint grid. HUD shows attempt, progress %, and touch **JUMP** / **◈ CYAN** buttons. Gates and orbs appear with beat-linked floor glow; the experience is playable but reads more **“clean arcade”** than **synthwave neon**.

Around **20–25%** progress (~1,200 m before the color wall), a small **section cue** appears: “COLOR WALL AHEAD”. There is still **no motion or camera change** tied to that warning.

## At the vertical transition (before fixes)

When world distance crosses **5,000**, the game **cuts instantly** from the horizontal scroller to a full-screen **color wall** lane:

- Background snaps to flat `#04091a` (horizontal parallax and floor vanish).
- Jump is disabled; only color switching steers lateral movement.
- The **first blocking gate** was only **~1.25 s** of travel away at current speed.
- **49%** of naive runs (no switching) died within **2 s** in simulation across 100 seeds.

So the owner's read is accurate: the idea is clever, but the handoff feels like a **sudden death wall** without teach, animation, or runway.

## Why it feels unfun

| Area | Problem |
|------|---------|
| Transition | Hard cut, no slow-mo, no gate/rotate telegraph, tutorial only in menus |
| Color wall | Immediate lethal gate, steep speed, weak neon compared to horizontal |
| Readability | Vertical lane loses horizon/grid; hazards compete with wash |
| Stakes | Failing the tutorial segment erases a long horizontal investment |
| Juice | Limited screen shake / flash on mode change; vertical has no trail |

## Fix plan (this PR)

1. **Telegraphed approach** — Portal arches on the runway, pulsing arrows, extended section cue, **~1.3 s warp** with slow-mo and canvas rotate before vertical sim starts.
2. **Teach once in-run** — Short banner: “Gravity turns! Tap color to steer.” stored in `localStorage` after first display.
3. **Safe runway** — **2.2 s** collision grace after entry; **speed ramp** in vertical; first lethal gate pushed to **≥850** course units with gentler early spacing; passive tutorial markers.
4. **Neon vertical** — Moving synth grid, horizon glow, hazard bloom, runner trail, arrow cues on passive gates.
5. **Measure** — Automated test: optimal bot survives first **3 s** of vertical at **100%**; naive survival **≥75%** across seeds after grace + spacing.
6. **Mobile & CI** — Keep Playwright matrix; capture before/after screenshots and clips as artifacts.

## Success criteria

- First vertical transition **readable** without reading the menu essay.
- Casual first attempt **usually survives** the opening color wall.
- Visual identity reads **neon synthwave** on phone and desktop without hiding hazards.
